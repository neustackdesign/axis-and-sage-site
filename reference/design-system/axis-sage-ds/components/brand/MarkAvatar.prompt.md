MarkAvatar — the quieter version of the logomark: the ribbons' intersection inside a Midnight circle.

- Use where the full Lockup is too loud or too small: social avatars, Instagram story bubbles, highlight covers, stationery seals, "by Axis & Sage" author chips.
- Never alongside the full Lockup in the same cluster. Never recolour; the ground is Midnight (default) or Cloud.
- Minimum 24px.
```jsx
<MarkAvatar size={40} />
```

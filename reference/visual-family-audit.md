# Axis & Sage visual-family audit

This audit records the read-only review of the three systems used for the authorised visual-family revision. The personal sites are reference systems, not pixel-parity targets. Axis & Sage remains its own strategy, design and growth consultancy.

## Tomiwa Ogunmodede portfolio

| Pattern | Classification | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analytical shell with compact navigation, large reading measure and clear page bands | ADAPT | Use the disciplined shell and content measure, while keeping Axis & Sage's dark opening and image-rich sections. |
| Helvetica/Geist-style sans for body and navigation | ADAPT | Axis & Sage keeps a controlled Helvetica Neue system stack for sans roles; Instrument Serif remains its display voice. |
| Mono metadata, tags and apparatus | ADOPT | Reuse a mono role for section numbers, metadata and project tags. |
| Thin dividers and work-row structure | ADOPT | Replace most rounded containers with hairline-led rows and editorial framing. |
| Work image preview and real case-study evidence | ADAPT | Keep Axis & Sage's project imagery and routes, adding editorial hierarchy without importing portfolio content. |
| RiseIn viewport reveal | ADAPT | Use a shared 18–24px rise with a restrained ease-out and reliable no-JS visibility. |
| PixelReveal image treatment | ADAPT | Use one controlled image mask/reveal for the hero and selected work, not a decorative effect on every image. |
| Lenis smooth scrolling | CONSIDER FOR OTHER SITES | Not required for Axis & Sage; native scrolling preserves anchors, Studio behaviour and reduced-motion support. |

## Shared-family conclusion

The revision adopts a common discipline rather than a shared skin: quiet paper surfaces, compact apparatus typography, hairline rules, controlled measures, fixed blurred navigation and deliberate motion. Axis & Sage remains image-rich and collaborative through its chess opening, logo, strategy/design/growth positioning, project evidence and Instrument Serif voice.
